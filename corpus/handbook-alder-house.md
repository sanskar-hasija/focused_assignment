## HB-A-1.1 Boilers
Each flat in Alder House has its own combi boiler, in the kitchen cupboard.

## HB-A-1.2 Stopcock
The stopcock is under the kitchen sink.

## HB-A-2.1 Low boiler pressure
If the boiler pressure gauge reads below 1 bar, you may top it up to 1.5 bar using the filling loop under the boiler. If the pressure drops below 1 bar again within a week, report it as a repair.

## HB-A-2.2 Tripped switch
If a switch trips at the consumer unit, you may reset it once. If it trips again, report it as a repair. A burning smell is an emergency: treat it as fire or smoke.

## HB-A-2.3 Cold radiators
If a radiator is cold at the top but warm at the bottom, it has air in it. Bleed it with a radiator key, then check the boiler pressure.
