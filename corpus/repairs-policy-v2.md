## POL2-1.1 Scope
This policy applies to every Harbour Lettings flat from 1 September 2025. It replaces the previous repairs policy (POL1) in full.

## POL2-2.1 Repairs the agency carries out
Harbour Lettings repairs the structure of the building, plumbing, heating and hot water, electrics, and gas appliances. We also repair locks on external doors and windows.

## POL2-2.2 Jobs tenants handle themselves
Tenants replace their own lightbulbs and smoke alarm batteries. If a sink is blocked through misuse, such as fat or food waste, please try a plunger first. Tenants should also keep air vents and extractor grilles clear.

## POL2-2.3 Damage caused by a tenant
If you or a visitor damaged something, please still report it. We will carry out the repair, and staff will decide whether to recharge the cost after an inspection.

## POL2-3.1 Emergencies
Emergencies are handled under the emergency procedure (EMG), which lists what counts as an emergency and what to do straight away. The repair targets below do not apply to them.

## POL2-3.2 Urgent repairs
We aim to fix urgent repairs within 24 hours. A repair is urgent if there is no heating or hot water between 1 October and 30 April, an external door or window will not lock, the only toilet in the flat is not working, or there is a continuous leak the tenant can contain, for example with a bucket.

## POL2-3.3 Routine repairs
We aim to fix routine repairs within 10 working days. Everything that is not an emergency or urgent is routine. This includes no heating or hot water between 1 May and 30 September.

## POL2-4.1 Problems that are already reported
If you report a problem that already has an open ticket, we won't open a second one. You'll be given the existing ticket reference instead.

## POL2-4.2 What the reporting system cannot do
The repairs reporting system cannot close tickets, add notes to tickets, change rent or promise an appointment time. Our repairs team will contact you to arrange a visit.
