## HB-B-1.1 Communal heating
Heating and hot water in Birch Court come from a communal plant room. Flats do not have their own boiler.

## HB-B-1.2 Plant room and radiators
Tenants must not enter the plant room, adjust any valves or bleed radiators. The system is pressurised centrally, so changes in one flat can affect the whole building.

## HB-B-1.3 Stopcock
The stopcock is in the hallway cupboard.

## HB-B-2.1 Heating and hot water faults
Report every heating or hot water fault as a repair, including cold radiators. How urgent it is follows the repairs policy (POL2).

## HB-B-2.2 Tripped switch
If a switch trips at the consumer unit, reset it once, as in any flat. If it trips again, report it as a repair.
