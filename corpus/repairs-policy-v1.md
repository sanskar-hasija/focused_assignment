## POL1-1.1 Scope
This policy was replaced by the Repairs policy v2 (POL2) on 1 September 2025. It is kept for reference only.

## POL1-2.1 Lightbulbs and smoke alarm batteries
The agency replaces smoke alarm batteries and lightbulbs in all flats on request.

## POL1-3.1 Heating faults
A flat with no heating is always an urgent repair, in any month of the year.
