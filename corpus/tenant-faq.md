## FAQ-1.1 Noise from neighbours
Noise is not a repair. Please email the housing officer at housing@harbourlettings.example.

## FAQ-1.2 Deposits
Your deposit is held by the deposit protection scheme, not by us directly. For questions about it, please ask our accounts team.

## FAQ-1.3 Bills and rent
Gas and electricity bills come from your energy supplier, not from Harbour Lettings. Rent questions go to accounts@harbourlettings.example.

## FAQ-1.4 Bins and parking
Bin collection days are posted on the noticeboard in each building's entrance hall. Parking permits are issued by the local council, not by the agency.
