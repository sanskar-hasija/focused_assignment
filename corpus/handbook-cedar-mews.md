## HB-C-1.1 Boilers and radiators
Each flat in Cedar Mews has its own boiler, and the same self-help applies as in Alder House. If the boiler pressure is below 1 bar, you may top it up to 1.5 bar with the filling loop; if it drops again within a week, report it as a repair. Radiators that are cold at the top can be bled with a radiator key.

## HB-C-1.2 Stopcock
The stopcock is in the utility cupboard.

## HB-C-2.1 Basement store flooding
The basement store can flood in heavy rain. If the water is rising or spreading, treat it as uncontrolled flooding, which is an emergency. A damp patch on the floor or walls is a routine repair.
