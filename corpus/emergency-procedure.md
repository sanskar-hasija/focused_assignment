## EMG-1.1 What counts as an emergency
There are five emergencies: a smell of gas; a carbon monoxide alarm going off, or symptoms such as headache, dizziness or nausea near a gas appliance; fire or smoke; water near electrics; and uncontrolled flooding.

## EMG-2.1 Gas smell
Do not use light switches, plugs or naked flames. Open doors and windows, and turn off the gas at the meter if it is safe to do so. Leave the flat and call the National Gas Emergency number on 0800 111 999.

## EMG-2.2 Carbon monoxide
Get everyone out into fresh air and do not go back inside. Call 0800 111 999. If anyone has symptoms, get medical help straight away.

## EMG-2.3 Fire or smoke
Get everyone out, closing doors behind you, and call 999. Do not go back in for belongings.

## EMG-2.4 Water near electrics
Do not touch switches, sockets or light fittings that are wet or near water. Turn off the power at the consumer unit only if it is dry and safe to reach. Keep everyone clear of the area.

## EMG-2.5 Uncontrolled flooding
Turn off the water at the stopcock; your building handbook says where it is. Keep away from electrics.

## EMG-3.1 Paging the on-call manager
Creating an emergency ticket pages the on-call manager automatically, at any time of day or night.
